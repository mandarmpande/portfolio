Operating Model - How I Approach Complex Programmes

My focus is not simply activity tracking. It is creating enough structure and transparency for different teams to make decisions at the right time.

  • Make the intake executable: Clarify what is entering delivery, what is missing, and what decisions are required before work begins.
  • Make dependencies visible: Connect application, infrastructure, security, data, business, and vendor dependencies rather than managing them as isolated workstreams.
  • Create decision transparency: Give stakeholders a shared view of risks, readiness, ownership, and escalation rather than relying on status reporting alone.
  • Protect delivery quality: Use readiness criteria, testing discipline, and structured defect resolution to reduce avoidable late-cycle surprises.
  • Stay technically credible: Understand enough of the technical landscape to challenge assumptions, coordinate specialists, and make informed delivery decisions.
  • Design for adoption: A successful enterprise change is not only technically released; users, governance, and operational ownership must be ready.
